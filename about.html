Oriflame Abuja operates as the crown jewel of Swedish beauty in Northern Nigeria. Headquartered in our Maitama beauty studio, with active consultation lounges across Wuse II, Gwarinpa, Asokoro, and the Central Business District (CBD), our mission is twofold:

First, providing Abuja clients with tailored beauty regimens formulated for our unique tropical climate and rich melanin-rich complexions. Second, building a formidable community of over 500+ independent beauty entrepreneurs who have built sustainable businesses through Oriflame’s global network.
